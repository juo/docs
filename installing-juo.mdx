Juo is a Shopify app. You install it from the Shopify App Store and pay for it through your Shopify bill.

## Before you start

- You need a Shopify store and permission to install apps on it.
- Subscriptions need a payment gateway that supports recurring payments. See Shopify's [subscription considerations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/purchase-options/subscriptions/considerations) for the supported gateways.

## Install the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Juo on the Shopify App Store">
    Go to [Juo on the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/juo-subscriptions) and select **Install**. Log in to Shopify if you're asked to, and choose the store you want to install Juo on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the permissions">
    Shopify shows the data Juo needs access to, such as products and orders. Juo uses it to create subscription plans and turn orders into subscriptions. Select **Install** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Pick the plan that fits your store. Both plans start with a 14-day free trial. The [App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/juo-subscriptions) shows the current prices and what each plan includes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the charge">
    Shopify asks you to approve the plan's recurring charge. Review it and select **Approve**. Charges appear on your Shopify bill, not on a separate invoice from Juo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Juo">
    Juo opens in your Shopify admin. You can always find it again under **Apps** in Shopify.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Not sure which plan fits your store, or need something custom? [Book a call](https://juo.com/meet) with our team to talk about pricing and plans.
</Tip>
