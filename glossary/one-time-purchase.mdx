## What is a one-time purchase

A one-time purchase, sometimes shortened to OTP, is a regular order. It's paid and shipped once, unlike a [subscription order](/glossary/subscription-order), which renews automatically.

## One-time purchases and subscriptions together

Most subscription stores offer both on the product page: **Buy once** and **Subscribe and save**. Customers can also mix both in one cart, which affects how shipping is charged. See [Shipping setup](/shipping-setup).

Subscribers can sometimes add one-time products to their next renewal, which raises [average order value](/glossary/average-order-value).
