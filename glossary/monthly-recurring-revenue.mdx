## What is MRR

Monthly recurring revenue, or MRR, is the predictable revenue your active [subscriptions](/glossary/subscription) generate each month. It leaves out one-time orders. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) is the same figure for a year, or MRR × 12.

## How to calculate MRR

Convert each active subscription to a monthly amount, then add them up:

- €30 every month = €30 MRR
- €30 every 2 weeks ≈ €65 MRR
- €80 every 3 months ≈ €26.70 MRR

Paused subscriptions don't count until they're resumed.

## What moves MRR

- **Up**: new subscribers, upsells and higher [average order value](/glossary/average-order-value).
- **Down**: [churn](/glossary/churn-rate), discounts and downgrades.
