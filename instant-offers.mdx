| **Who it's for** | Brands running a sale or limited offer that existing subscribers can use before their next renewal |
| **Goal** | Earn extra revenue from subscribers during a campaign, without changing their subscription |
| **Primary metric** | Share of eligible subscribers who place an instant order |
| **Supporting metrics** | Revenue per campaign, average instant order value, cancellations during and after the campaign |

## Why it matters

A regular [upsell](/upsells-for-subscriptions) adds a product to the next renewal. That works when the next renewal is close, but a sale has an end date. A subscriber whose renewal is three weeks away would see the offer expire first.

An instant offer removes that wait. The subscriber buys the products now, the order ships on its own, and the subscription keeps its schedule, products and price.

## Plan the offer

Decide four things before you set it up:

- **Products.** Choose products subscribers already know, or a short list of bestsellers. A short row is easier to scan than a full catalog.
- **Discount.** An instant offer takes one discount, set as a percentage, an amount or a fixed price. It applies once, to the separate order. Subscription discounts don't apply to it.
- **Dates.** Set a start and end date for a campaign. Subscribers see when it ends during its last 7 days.
- **Limits.** Decide whether a subscriber can buy repeatedly, once, or each product once.

<Note>
  An instant offer is charged to the subscription's saved payment method. Only subscribers with an active subscription and a payment method can buy it.
</Note>

## Set it up in Juo

### Create the offer

1. Go to **Experiences → Upsells → Add Upsell**.
2. Enter a **Campaign name**.
3. Under **Upsell type**, select **Buy now as a separate order**.
4. Set the **Schedule**: always available, or between a start and end date.
5. Optionally add a **Discount**. Choose **Percentage**, **Amount** or **Fixed price**.
6. Choose a **Usage limit**:
   - **No limits**: customers can buy as often as they like while the campaign is active.
   - **Once per customer**: customers can buy one product from the campaign, once.
   - **Each product once per customer**: customers can buy each offer product once.
7. Optionally limit **Customer eligibility** to specific segments or to subscribers of a specific product.
8. Select **Save**, then add the **Products**.
9. Turn on **Active**.

See [Upsells](/upsells) for how a buy-now upsell differs from one added to the subscription.

### Check the Instant product sale block

Subscribers buy from the **Instant product sale** block in the Customer Portal. Juo adds it to the main subscription page, below the header, and it stays hidden until a buy-now upsell is live.

Each block sells one campaign. By default it sells your oldest buy-now upsell that is active and inside its schedule. If you run more than one campaign, select the one a block sells under **Upsell campaign** in the [Editor](/editor).

In the Editor you can also change the block's theme, product card style and promo badge, and reword its header in the **Translations** tab. Use **Preview customer** to see the block as a real subscriber does.

### Test the offer

1. Open the Customer Portal as a test subscriber who matches the campaign's eligibility.
2. Select **Buy now** on a product, or select several and choose **Review and pay**.
3. Select **Place order**.
4. In Shopify, check that the order is separate from the subscription, the product line reads **One-time purchase**, and the discount and shipping match what you set.
5. Open the subscription in Juo and check that it didn't change.

## What subscribers see

The block shows the campaign's discount as a badge, for example "20% off", and the products with their offer prices. Subscribers review the order, confirm it, and Juo charges their payment method right away. The block then shows the order as placed.

If an order can't be placed, the block explains why. A declined payment, an unpaid earlier order, an offer that has ended and too many orders in a short time each show their own message.

## Guardrails

- **Check stock** before the campaign starts. Products that are no longer available can't be ordered.
- **Use usage limits** for scarce or heavily discounted products.
- **Avoid overlapping campaigns** that share products. A block without a selected campaign sells your oldest live one.
- **Check shipping** on a test order. The instant order ships separately from the subscription.
- **End dates are final.** A subscriber who opens the block after the campaign ends sees no offer.

## How to measure it

- **Instant orders** placed during the campaign, and the share of eligible subscribers who placed one.
- **Revenue and average order value** of instant orders compared with renewals in the same period.
- **Cancellations** during and after the campaign, compared with the weeks before it.
- **Repeat purchases.** Subscribers who buy an instant offer product more than once are good candidates for adding it to their subscription.
