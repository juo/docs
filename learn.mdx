Playbooks explain how to solve a subscription business problem: why it happens, which options you have, and how to measure the result. They don't depend on product features, so they stay valid when Juo changes.

Each playbook ends with **How to do this in Juo**: links to the recipes and features that implement it today. Recipes are marked **Recipe** in the sidebar.

## How the guides fit together

| Guide type | Answers | Where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Playbook** | Why and what: the problem, the strategy, the trade-offs | Playbooks |
| **Recipe** | How to build one playbook in Juo today, often from a blueprint | Playbooks, next to the playbook it implements |
| **Feature reference** | What a feature does, its settings and limits | Product |
| **Setup guide** | Getting your store live with Juo | Get started |
| **Troubleshooting** | Why something happened and how to fix it | Help |
