## Why it matters

A gift is often cheaper than a discount. It costs you the product, not the full price, and a subscriber remembers a surprise in the box longer than a few euros off. Gifts also introduce products subscribers haven't tried, so some become paid add-ons later.

The risk is giving too much, to the wrong people or at the wrong time. A gift works when it lands just before a subscriber would otherwise leave, or when it rewards behaviour you want more of.

## Gift strategies

| Strategy | When the gift lands | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Welcome gift** | The first order or first renewal | Making the first weeks feel special, when churn is highest |
| **Milestone gift** | Set renewals, such as the 3rd, 6th or 12th | Giving subscribers a reason to stay until the next reward |
| **Anniversary gift** | One year after subscribing | Thanking long-term subscribers |
| **Save offer** | When a subscriber tries to cancel | Keeping subscribers who are unsure, instead of a discount |
| **Claimable gift** | When the subscriber chooses, from the portal or an email | Rewards subscribers pick themselves, campaigns with limited stock |
| **Gift with a bigger order** | When the next order passes a value or size | Raising order value, for example in build-a-box |
| **Seasonal gift** | One planned renewal, such as Black Friday or the holidays | Campaigns for existing subscribers |
| **Surprise and delight** | Any order, chosen by your team | Fixing a bad experience, rewarding reviews or referrals |
| **Win-back gift** | When a cancelled subscriber returns | Giving former subscribers a reason to come back |

<Tip>
  Not sure where to start? A milestone gift just before the renewal where your retention drops most is usually the best first gift. Check your cohort retention in [Analytics](/analytics).
</Tip>

### Choosing the gift

- **Something they don't already get.** Samples and new products cost little and may become paid add-ons.
- **Something that fits the box.** Small, light and quick to pack, so it doesn't change shipping costs.
- **A choice, where you can.** Letting subscribers pick between two or three gifts makes the gift feel more personal.

## Guardrails

- **Measure against a holdout.** Keep a group without the gift, or you won't know whether it kept anyone.
- **One gift per moment.** Check that automatic gifts don't stack, for example a milestone gift and a seasonal gift in the same order.
- **Watch stock.** An automatic gift keeps being added while the rule is active.
- **Make it visible.** Say in the renewal email and the portal that a gift is coming. A gift subscribers don't notice doesn't keep them.

## How to measure it

- **Retention** of gifted subscribers against the holdout, at the renewals right after the gift.
- **Cost per retained subscriber**: total gift cost divided by the extra subscribers kept.
- **Conversion to paid**: how many subscribers add the gifted product later.

## How to add gifts in Juo

There's a way to add a gift for each situation: automatically on set renewals, as an offer in the portal, from an email, by your team, or through the API.

### Automatically, on set renewals

| Method | Use it for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Extras](/extras) | Free product, free shipping or an extra discount on set renewals of a subscription plan | Set once per plan. Can't be added to the first order, and changes apply to new subscriptions only. |
| [Custom workflows](/custom-workflows) | A fixed sequence of orders with a free product on chosen cycles, such as a welcome kit | Built per product with cycle rules. |
| [Workflows](/workflows/workflows-overview), **Next order updated** trigger | Rules such as "add a gift to the 6th renewal" or "add a gift when the order passes €60" | Use the **Add Item** action on the upcoming order. Start from the **Gift flow** [blueprint](/workflows/blueprints), which includes a holdout group. See [VIP rewards](/guides/vip-rewards). |

### As an offer in the Customer Portal

| Method | Use it for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Workflows](/workflows/actions) offer actions | A gift as a save offer when a subscriber cancels, or a choice of gifts | Use **Offer Free Product** or **Offer Selection**. See the [cancellation flow guide](/guides/cancellation-flow). |
| [Upsells](/upsells) | Gifts subscribers claim themselves, with dates, eligibility and a usage limit | Set the discount so the product is free or nearly free, then test it with a test subscriber. |

### From an email or text

| Method | Use it for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Quick actions](/quick-actions), Add Product | A one-click "Add your gift to your next delivery" button in Klaviyo | Works with products in an upsell campaign. See [Offer upsells in the portal and by email](/guides/upsells-portal-and-email). |

### By your team

| Method | Use it for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One subscription**, in Juo | Surprise and delight, or making up for a problem | Open the subscription and add the product to the next order as a one-time item at the price you choose. |
| [Bulk actions](/bulk-actions), Add product | A seasonal gift for many subscribers at once | Filter the subscriptions, add the product as **one-time** with a **custom price**. See [Set up a campaign for subscribers](/guides/seasonal-campaigns-in-juo#add-a-gift-to-the-next-order). |
| [Bulk actions](/bulk-actions), Run workflow | A gift with conditions, run on a selection | Runs your **Admin initiated** workflow on the selected subscriptions. |

### Through the API or AI assistants

| Method | Use it for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Juo API](https://juo.dev/) | Gifts triggered from your own systems, such as a loyalty or referral app | Add an item to a subscription with a custom price. |
| [Juo MCP](/use-juo-with-ai) | Adding gifts by asking an AI assistant | For one-off requests, such as "add a free sample to these 20 subscriptions". |

<Note>
  Gift subscriptions, where someone pays for a subscription for another person, are a different setup. See [prepaid subscription](/glossary/prepaid-subscription).
</Note>
