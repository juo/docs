Skipping the next order so the subscription renews one cycle later.

</Info>

## What is skipping a renewal

When a subscriber skips, their next [renewal](/glossary/subscription-renewal) doesn't happen. They aren't charged, nothing ships and the subscription continues on the following date. A subscriber on a monthly plan who skips in March gets their next order in April.

## Skip vs pause

- A **skip** misses one order and the subscription carries on.
- A [pause](/glossary/subscription-pause) stops orders until the subscriber or merchant resumes it.

## Why it matters

Subscribers with too much product often cancel. Letting them skip instead keeps them subscribed, which is why skip is a common offer in a [cancellation flow](/glossary/retention-flow).

## Skipping in Juo

Subscribers skip in the [Customer Portal](/customer-portal). You control whether they can in **Settings** → **Customer Portal**.
