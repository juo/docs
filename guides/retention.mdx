## How retention moves subscription revenue

Subscription revenue depends on a few numbers:

- **Recurring revenue** = active subscribers × average order value × orders per subscriber in a period.
- **Lifetime value** ≈ average order value × gross margin × paid orders before a subscriber leaves.
- **Paid orders per subscriber** grow when fewer subscribers cancel, skip, pause or fail payment at each renewal.

Small changes compound. A subscriber kept for one more renewal adds a full order of revenue, and a subscriber kept past an early drop-off point is likely to stay for several more.

## Retention by subscriber stage

Subscribers leave at different moments and for different reasons. Review each stage and the guide that covers it.

| Stage | What to manage | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First orders** | New subscribers reach their second and third orders | [Fight new subscriber churn](/guides/new-subscriber-churn) |
| **Every renewal** | Subscribers skip, delay or adjust an order instead of cancelling | [Renewal reminders](/guides/renewal-reminders) |
| **Failed payments** | Payments that fail are retried and recovered before the subscription stops | [Recover failed payments](/guides/failed-payments) |
| **Cancellation** | Subscribers who try to leave stay on terms that fit them better | [Cancellation flow](/guides/cancellation-flow) |
| **Long-term subscribers** | High-value subscribers have a reason to stay to the next milestone | [VIP rewards](/guides/vip-rewards) · [Gifts for subscribers](/guides/gifts) |
| **After cancellation** | Cancelled subscribers return without new acquisition cost | [Win back cancelled subscribers](/guides/win-back) |

Find where your subscribers leave in **Analytics** → **Cohort retention**, and start with the stage that loses the most. See [Analytics](/analytics).

## Principles for every retention guide

- **Lead with value, then offer.** Content that reminds subscribers why they subscribed costs nothing. Incentives cost margin and teach subscribers to ask for them.
- **Give subscribers control.** Skips, pauses, swaps and frequency changes keep subscribers who would otherwise cancel. Check which ones you allow in **Settings** → **Customer Portal**.
- **Limit every incentive.** A subscriber shouldn't be able to collect the same offer again and again.
- **Test before you scale.** Compare offers or messages on a share of subscribers before you settle on one.
- **Measure the renewals after the save.** A save that cancels at the next renewal only delays churn.
