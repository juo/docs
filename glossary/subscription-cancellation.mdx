## What is a subscription cancellation

When a [subscription](/glossary/subscription) is cancelled, no more [renewals](/glossary/subscription-renewal) happen. Past orders aren't affected.

## Two kinds of cancellation

- **Voluntary**: the subscriber cancels, usually in the [customer portal](/glossary/customer-portal), or asks support to.
- **Involuntary**: the subscription stops because payments failed and [dunning](/glossary/dunning) couldn't recover them.

Both count towards your [churn rate](/glossary/churn-rate).

## How to reduce cancellations

Offer a [skip](/glossary/skip-renewal), [pause](/glossary/subscription-pause) or [product swap](/glossary/product-swap) in a [cancellation flow](/glossary/retention-flow) before the cancellation goes through. To bring back subscribers who left, see [win-back](/glossary/win-back).

## Cancellations in Juo

Cancelled subscriptions can be reactivated if you allow it in **Settings** → **Customer Portal**. See [Customer Portal](/customer-portal).
