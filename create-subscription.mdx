---
title: "Create a subscription"
description: "Manually create a subscription for a customer in the Admin Panel."
---

You can create a subscription without a storefront checkout from the **Subscriptions** list.

1. Go to **Subscriptions**.
2. Click **Create subscription**.
3. Complete the steps: **Customer** → **Billing & payment** → **Products** → **Address** → **Shipping**.
4. Confirm to create the subscription.

You must select a payment method when the shop has payment methods available. Parcel-locker / pickup-point shipping can be selected on the shipping step when your store supports it.

## Choose a subscription plan or a custom frequency

On the **Billing & Payment** step, under **Billing frequency**, choose how the subscription is billed:

- **Subscription plan**: click **Select subscription plan** and pick one of your existing plans. The subscription takes the plan's billing and delivery frequency, and the plan's discount applies to every product. On the **Products** step, you can only add the products and variants that plan includes.
- **Custom frequency**: set the billing frequency yourself. The subscription isn't linked to a plan, so products are added at full price.

If you switch to a plan from a different plan group, the products you already picked are cleared. The summary shows the chosen plan as **Subscription plan**.

After creation, manage the subscription like any other, including [bulk actions](/bulk-actions) and Customer Portal access.
