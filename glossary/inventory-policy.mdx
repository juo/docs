## What is an out-of-stock policy

An out-of-stock policy, or inventory policy, is the rule for [renewals](/glossary/subscription-renewal) that include a product you can't ship right now.

## Common options

- **Renew anyway**: the order is created and paid, and ships when stock arrives, like a backorder.
- **Skip or delay the renewal** until the product is back.
- **Remove or swap the product** and renew the rest of the order.

Whichever you choose, tell subscribers what's happening. A surprise delay is a common reason to cancel.
