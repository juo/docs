In many markets, customers prefer to pay with a local method instead of a card. Juo lets them subscribe with the payment method they already use, including:

- Klarna
- iDEAL
- Bancontact
- BLIK
- MB WAY
- and more

Offering the payment method customers expect at checkout can raise the share of orders that start a subscription.

## Request access

Local payment methods aren't turned on by default. Each store needs a short setup with the Juo team, based on your markets and payment provider.

To request access, email [support@juo.io](mailto:support@juo.io) or message us through the chat in the Juo app. Tell us which payment methods and countries you want to offer.
