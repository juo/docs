An experience decides how customers subscribe and what they receive with each renewal. Pick the one that fits what you sell. You can run more than one on the same store.

## Choose an experience

| Experience | Best for | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Subscribe and save](/subscribe-and-save) | Products customers reorder: coffee, supplements, pet food, skincare | Customers subscribe on the product page, choose a delivery frequency and get a subscription discount. |
| [Build-a-box](/build-a-box) | Stores with many flavors, scents or variants | Customers fill their own box and unlock a bigger discount as the box grows. |
| [Curated boxes](/curated-boxes) | Mystery boxes, clubs and monthly drops | You choose what goes into each delivery. Subscribers receive the box on a schedule you set. |
| [Memberships](/memberships) | Perks instead of products | Customers pay for a club with benefits such as free shipping or member discounts. |

Most stores start with **Subscribe and save**. To bill for several deliveries at once, add a [prepaid](/glossary/prepaid-subscription) purchase option to a subscription plan.

## Go beyond the basics with workflows

Many subscription experiences start from Subscribe and save and add rules on top. Build these with [Workflows](/workflows/workflows-overview), or start from a ready-made [blueprint](/workflows/blueprints):

- **Starter kit, then refills**: send a starter kit first and swap it for refills from the next order. Use the **Starter kit → refill swap** blueprint.
- **Rewards for staying**: add a free gift on selected renewals or raise the discount with every renewal. See [VIP rewards](/vip-rewards) and [Gifts for subscribers](/gifts).
- **A guided first month**: welcome new subscribers and show a different message after each of their first orders. See [Fight new subscriber churn](/new-subscriber-churn).

For more, browse [Guides](/guides-overview).

## Before you sell your first subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your experience">
    Create a subscription plan, box or membership using the pages above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show it on your store">
    Add the [subscription widget](/adding-juo-subscription-widget) to your product page. For Build-a-box, add the [box page](/build-a-box-storefront).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give subscribers a way to manage it">
    Add the [Customer Portal link](/linking-customer-portal) to your store, so subscribers can skip, swap or pause without contacting you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it yourself">
    Subscribe as a customer and go through the whole journey. See [Test as your first subscriber](/test-first-subscriber).
  </Step>
</Steps>

  Moving subscribers from another app? See [Migrate to Juo](/migrating-to-juo). Not sure which experience fits your products? Email [support@juo.io](mailto:support@juo.io) or message us in the in-app chat.
</Note>
