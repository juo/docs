Global settings live under **Settings** in the Admin Panel. This page covers the **General** tab. Related options also appear under **Customer Portal**, **Retention and acquisition**, **Notifications**, and **Integrations**.

## Out of stock items

Choose what happens when a subscription renews with an out-of-stock item:

- **Wait until everything is in stock**: Hold the renewal until the items are back in stock, swapped or removed. Juo keeps retrying for the length of your dunning period.
- **Renew with out-of-stock items**: Renew and bill without interruption, including the out-of-stock items.
- **Renew with in-stock items only**: Charge and ship the in-stock items on schedule. Juo skips the out-of-stock items and includes them in the next regular order.

### Renew with in-stock items only

When you select this option, set a **Minimum in-stock share of the order value** from 1 to 100% (50% by default). If the in-stock items make up less than this share of the order's value, the renewal waits for full stock instead. Free items, such as gifts, don't count toward the share.

The customer pays for the shipped items what they would have cost on the full order. The renewal's entry on the subscription timeline lists the skipped items.

Juo waits for full stock instead of skipping items when:

- The order has only one product line, or every line is out of stock.
- An out-of-stock item is part of a box (Build-a-box, Dynamic box or Curated box).
- An out-of-stock item is a one-time product or a free gift.
- The subscription is prepaid (billed for several deliveries at once) or postpaid.

## Price changes

When product prices change in Shopify:

- **Update to the new price**: Upcoming renewals use the new catalog price.
- **Keep the initial price**: Existing subscription lines keep the price from when they were added.

Changes apply to upcoming orders only.

## One-time products price

Control whether selling-plan discounts apply when a customer adds a product as a **one-time** line in the Customer Portal. Enable this if one-time add-ons should receive the same subscription discount as recurring lines.

## Shipping rates

By default, Juo **retains** the shipping rate on a subscription until the customer updates it (for example by changing products or address).

Enable **Dynamic Shipping Rates** to pull rates from your Shopify shipping profile whenever rates change. Already-scheduled upcoming renewals keep their current rate; the new rates apply from the following cycle.

For profile setup details, see [Shipping setup](/shipping-setup).

## Tags

Define tags Juo writes to Shopify **orders** and **customers** (for example first order vs recurring, active vs canceled). Changes apply to upcoming orders only. Cycle and reference prefixes get an automatic index suffix.

## Copy initial order attributes

Enable this to copy selected custom attributes from the customer’s **first order** onto the subscription. This is useful when a shipping app adds pickup-point codes after checkout.

- Add the attribute **keys** to whitelist (only those keys are copied).
- Existing subscription attributes are **never overwritten**.

Pair this with pickup-point integrations when attributes must persist onto renewals. See [Shipping setup](/shipping-setup#choose-your-setup).

## Freeze renewals

**Freeze all renewals** stops subscription activity across your store:

- No renewal charges.
- No renewal orders created.
- Subscribers can't edit subscriptions in the Customer Portal.

Renewals that fall due while the freeze is on are retried about every 24 hours, and resume when you turn the freeze off. Use it for planned outages, catalog changes or emergencies. To stop one subscription, pause it instead.
