## What is a cancellation flow

A cancellation flow, also called a retention flow or cancel flow, starts when a [subscriber](/glossary/subscriber) tries to cancel. Before the [cancellation](/glossary/subscription-cancellation) goes through, it asks why they're leaving and offers an alternative that fits the reason.

## What a cancellation flow includes

- **A short survey**: the reason for cancelling, such as too much product, price or a bad experience.
- **Save offers** matched to the reason: a discount, a [skip](/glossary/skip-renewal), a [pause](/glossary/subscription-pause), a lower [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency) or a [product swap](/glossary/product-swap).
- **A clear way out**: subscribers who still want to leave can cancel without friction.

Survey answers also show you why subscribers leave, so you can fix the cause.

## Why it matters

A good cancellation flow saves a share of subscribers who would otherwise leave and lowers your [churn rate](/glossary/churn-rate).
