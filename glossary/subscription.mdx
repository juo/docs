## What is a subscription

In ecommerce, a subscription is a purchase that repeats automatically. The customer picks a [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency), pays at checkout and then receives and pays for each [renewal](/glossary/subscription-renewal) without ordering again. They manage it in a [customer portal](/glossary/customer-portal).

## Main types of subscriptions

- **Replenishment**: products customers run out of, such as coffee, supplements or pet food. Usually sold as [subscribe and save](/glossary/subscribe-and-save).
- **Curation**: a [subscription box](/glossary/subscription-box) chosen by the merchant or built by the customer.
- **Access**: a [membership](/glossary/membership) with perks instead of products.

Subscriptions can also be pay-as-you-go, billed per delivery, or [prepaid](/glossary/prepaid-subscription), billed for several deliveries at once.

## Why stores sell subscriptions

Subscriptions turn one-time buyers into recurring revenue, make demand easier to plan and raise [customer lifetime value](/glossary/lifetime-value).
