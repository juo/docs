The Juo team runs every migration for you. This page covers what we need from you and what to expect, whichever app you're moving from. To schedule yours, email [support@juo.io](mailto:support@juo.io).

## What your subscribers experience

Nothing changes for your subscribers. Their products, prices, discounts, delivery frequency and next order date stay the same.

- They don't reconfirm, resubscribe or re-enter payment details.
- They don't receive any notifications by default. You decide whether and when to tell them, for example to introduce your new [Customer Portal](/customer-portal).
- After the migration, they manage their subscriptions in the Juo Customer Portal.

## What we need from you

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Juo">
    [Install Juo](/installing-juo) from the Shopify App Store. You can set up your offers, widget and Customer Portal while we prepare the migration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give us access">
    Give the Juo team access to:

    - **Your Shopify store**: approve our collaborator request.
    - **The app you're migrating from**: invite [support@juo.io](mailto:support@juo.io) as a staff member or admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your subscription data">
    Depending on your current app, we'll ask for one of these:

    - **An API key or access token**, so we can read your data directly.
    - **A CSV export** of your customers and subscriptions.

    We need each subscriber's products, quantities, prices, discounts, delivery frequency, next order date, shipping address and payment method. Payment methods move as secure tokens. We never need full card numbers.

    If you store payment methods outside Shopify Payments, for example in Stripe or Braintree, we may also need access to that payment provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a mapping file (optional)">
    Skip this step if your subscriptions should move exactly as they are.

    You need a mapping file when anything should change during the migration. For example, you're retiring legacy products, merging variants or moving subscribers to new prices or frequencies. The file tells us how each old item becomes a new one.

    Use one row per old item:

    | Old product or variant | Old SKU | New variant ID | Quantity | Price | Frequency | Notes |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Coffee Blend 250g | CB-250 | 44012345678 | 1 | 18.00 | Every 4 weeks | Same as before |
    | Coffee Blend 500g (legacy) | CB-500-OLD | 44012345699 | 2 | 16.00 | Every 4 weeks | Becomes 2 × 250g |

    Leave a cell empty to keep the current value. We'll send you a summary of the changes to approve before we migrate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How migration day works

The migration itself takes a few hours, depending on how many subscriptions you have. We agree on the date and time with you in advance.

- **No double charges.** Subscriptions due to renew during the migration window are put on hold. They renew once, in Juo, right after the migration. They never renew in your old app and then again in Juo.
- **Your old app stops billing.** Once a subscription is in Juo, it no longer bills in your old app.

<Warning>
  Before migration day, turn off customer notifications in your old app. Otherwise it may email subscribers when their subscriptions are canceled there.
</Warning>

## After the migration

1. Check that the number of active subscriptions in Juo matches your old app.
2. Open a few subscribers and compare their products, prices and next order dates.
3. Replace links to your old app's customer portal, for example in your store's account page and emails.
4. Once everything checks out, uninstall your old app. Keep a final export for your records.

Questions about your migration? Contact [support@juo.io](mailto:support@juo.io).
