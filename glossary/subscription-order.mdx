The first order of a subscription, or an order created by a renewal.

</Info>

## What is a subscription order

A subscription order is any order that belongs to a [subscription](/glossary/subscription):

- **First order**: the customer picks the subscription option, chooses a [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency) and checks out.
- **Renewal orders**: created automatically on each [renewal](/glossary/subscription-renewal), paid with the saved payment method and shipped like a regular order.

## How renewal orders differ

Renewal orders are created without a checkout. Prices, discounts and shipping are calculated again for each one, so they can differ from the first order. See [Shipping setup](/shipping-setup).

## Subscription orders in Shopify

In Shopify, each renewal is a normal order linked to the subscriber's [subscription contract](/glossary/subscription-contract), so it goes through your usual fulfillment.
