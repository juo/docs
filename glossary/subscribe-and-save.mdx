## What is subscribe and save

Subscribe and save is the most common ecommerce [subscription](/glossary/subscription). On the product page, customers choose between a [one-time purchase](/glossary/one-time-purchase) and a subscription at a lower price, then pick a [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency). It suits products people run out of, such as coffee, supplements, skincare or pet food.

## How to set it up well

- **Discount**: usually 10–20%. Enough to be worth it, small enough to keep your margin on every renewal.
- **Frequencies**: two or three options based on how long the product lasts.
- **Flexibility**: let subscribers [skip](/glossary/skip-renewal), change dates and swap products, so they don't cancel when life changes.

A [variable discount](/glossary/variable-discount) can give a bigger discount on the first order or reward subscribers who stay.
