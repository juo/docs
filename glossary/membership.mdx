## What is a membership

A membership, or paid loyalty program, is a recurring fee that unlocks benefits rather than products: member-only prices, free shipping, early access to launches or exclusive products. Members keep the perks while the membership is active.

## Membership vs subscription

- A product [subscription](/glossary/subscription) delivers the same products on a schedule.
- A membership sells access to benefits. Members still buy products when they want.

Many brands combine both, for example by giving subscribers member perks.

## Why brands offer memberships

Memberships bring predictable revenue and give customers a reason to buy from you instead of elsewhere. They work best when the perks are worth more than the fee for your regular customers.
