## What is a subscription migration

A subscription migration moves your existing [subscriptions](/glossary/subscription) to a new subscription app. Done well, subscribers don't notice: they don't sign up again, re-enter payment details or get unexpected emails.

## What moves in a migration

- **Subscribers**: name, email and delivery address.
- **Subscriptions**: products, quantities, prices, discounts, [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency) and next order date.
- **Payment methods**: moved as secure tokens, never as card numbers.

If products change during the move, a mapping file matches old [SKUs](/glossary/stock-keeping-unit) or variants to new ones.
