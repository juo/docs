Putting a subscription on hold without cancelling it.

</Info>

## What is a subscription pause

A paused [subscription](/glossary/subscription) doesn't renew: no charges and no orders. It stays in place with its products and settings, and starts renewing again when the subscriber or merchant resumes it. Some pauses have an end date and resume automatically.

## Pause vs skip vs cancel

- [Skip](/glossary/skip-renewal): misses one order.
- **Pause**: stops orders until resumed.
- [Cancel](/glossary/subscription-cancellation): ends the subscription.

## Why offer a pause

Subscribers who travel, have too much product or need a break often cancel when a pause would do. Offering one, especially in a [cancellation flow](/glossary/retention-flow), keeps them subscribed.

## Pausing in Juo

Subscribers pause and resume in the [Customer Portal](/customer-portal). You control whether they can in **Settings** → **Customer Portal**.
