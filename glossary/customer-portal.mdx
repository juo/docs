## What is a customer portal

A customer portal, or subscription portal, is a self-service page where [subscribers](/glossary/subscriber) manage their [subscriptions](/glossary/subscription) without contacting support. They usually open it from the store's account page or from a link in an email.

## What subscribers can do there

- [Skip](/glossary/skip-renewal) or reschedule the next order, or renew now
- Change the [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency)
- [Pause](/glossary/subscription-pause), resume or [cancel](/glossary/subscription-cancellation)
- Add, remove or [swap](/glossary/product-swap) products
- Update the delivery address and payment method

## Why it matters

Subscribers who can skip, pause or swap are less likely to cancel. A good portal lowers support load and [churn](/glossary/churn-rate), and gives space for upsells.
