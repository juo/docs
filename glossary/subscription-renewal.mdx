The moment a subscription charges the subscriber and creates the next order.

</Info>

## What is a renewal

A renewal happens on each scheduled date of a [subscription](/glossary/subscription). The saved payment method is charged and a new [subscription order](/glossary/subscription-order) is created. The next renewal date is then set from the [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency).

## What can change a renewal

- The subscriber [skips](/glossary/skip-renewal), reschedules or [pauses](/glossary/subscription-pause).
- The subscriber or merchant chooses **Renew now**, which renews straight away.
- The payment fails, which starts [dunning](/glossary/dunning).
- A product is unavailable. See [out-of-stock policy](/glossary/inventory-policy).

## Renewals in Juo

Each renewal creates a regular Shopify order. Subscribers manage their next renewal in the [Customer Portal](/customer-portal).
