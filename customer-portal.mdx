The Customer Portal is where subscribers manage their subscriptions without contacting you. It lives on your store's domain at `/apps/juo`, and you decide what subscribers can change there.

## How subscribers get in

Subscribers open the portal from the **Manage subscriptions** link in your store. To add it, see [Customer Portal link](/linking-customer-portal).

They don't need a store account. They can log in with:

- their Shopify customer account,
- a one-time code sent to their email,
- Google or Facebook, if you turn social login on.

## What subscribers can do

| Area | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| **Next order** | Renew now, skip the next order, change the renewal date |
| **Subscription** | Change the delivery frequency, pause, resume, cancel, reactivate |
| **Products** | Change quantity or variant, swap, add, remove, skip one product in the next order, add a discount code |
| **Details** | Change the delivery address, delivery method or pickup point, update the payment method |

Changes apply from the next renewal. A payment method update sends a secure link to the subscriber's email.

## Choose what subscribers can change

In Juo, go to **Settings** → **Customer Portal** to turn each action on or off:

- **Management actions**: cancel, pause, skip, change renewal date, change frequency (including a custom frequency), change address and delivery method, redeem discount codes, reactivate cancelled or failed subscriptions, add products as one-time purchases.
- **Product editing**: add, remove, swap, change quantity or variant.
- **Excluded products**: hide products from the portal. Turn on **auto-exclude new products** if you add products often.

Anything you turn off, subscribers need to ask your support team for.

<Note>
  If a plan has a minimum number of renewals, subscribers can't cancel, pause or skip until they reach it. The portal tells them how many renewals are left.
</Note>

Turn on **redeem discount codes** only if you share codes that work on subscriptions. See [Discounts](/discounts).

## Make it look like your store

The portal picks up your store's fonts and colors. Under **Settings** → **Customer Portal**, you can fine-tune colors and button shapes.

For more control, turn on the **block-based Customer Portal** in the same settings. You then design the portal in the [Editor](/editor): reorder sections, add banners and upsells, change texts and translations, and preview everything with a real subscriber's data before you publish.

<Tip>
  Added custom links or code to your current portal? Contact [team@juo.io](mailto:team@juo.io) before you switch, so we can move them to blocks for you.
</Tip>
