Before real customers subscribe, go through the journey yourself: subscribe, find the subscription in Juo, and manage it as a customer would. It takes about 15 minutes and catches most launch issues.

## Before you start

- You've created a subscription offer, for example [Subscribe and save](/subscribe-and-save).
- The [subscription widget](/adding-juo-subscription-widget) is on your product page.
- The [Manage subscriptions link](/linking-customer-portal) is in your store.

## Walk through the journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe on your store">
    Open the product page, choose the subscription option in the widget and complete checkout. Use a payment method that supports subscriptions, and an order you can refund afterwards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the order and the subscription">
    The order appears in Shopify under **Orders**. In Juo, go to **Subscriptions** and open the new subscription. Check the products, price, discount, delivery frequency and next order date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Customer Portal as the customer">
    Use the **Manage subscriptions** link in your store, or open the subscription in Juo and select **Generate customer portal link** to see the portal exactly as the customer does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try what subscribers will do">
    Skip the next order, change the delivery frequency, then start to cancel. If you've set up a [cancellation flow](/guides/cancel-flow-blueprint), it appears at this point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the emails">
    Review the emails you received at each step. Adjust them under **Settings → Notifications**. See [Notifications](/notifications).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clean up">
    Cancel the test subscription and refund the order in Shopify.
  </Step>
</Steps>
