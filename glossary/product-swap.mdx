## What is a product swap

A product swap replaces a product in a [subscription](/glossary/subscription) with another product or variant, for example a different flavor or size. The rest of the subscription stays the same.

## Who swaps and why

- **Subscribers** swap to try something new or because they have too much of one product. Offering a swap instead of a cancellation keeps them subscribed.
- **Merchants** swap products in bulk when a product is discontinued, renamed or replaced by a new version.

## Product swaps in Juo

Subscribers swap in the [Customer Portal](/customer-portal). To swap many subscriptions at once, use [Bulk actions](/bulk-actions).
