Juo doesn't have its own shipping rates. Subscription orders use the shipping profiles you set up in Shopify under **Settings** → **Shipping and delivery**. Most stores need no extra setup. If subscriptions should ship differently from one-time purchases, the Juo team sets it up with you.

## How Shopify handles subscription shipping

- **Same rates by default.** A product ships at the same rate whether it's bought once or as a subscription.
- **Subscriptions can have their own rates.** Shopify can link a separate shipping profile to your subscription plans, for example for free subscription shipping. These profiles are created through the API, so we create them for you.
- **Mixed carts are split.** When a cart has both one-time and subscription items, checkout shows the shipping for each part separately. The customer pays for both.
- **Subscriptions are grouped.** Several subscription items with the same delivery frequency share one shipping charge.
- **Checkout explains recurring shipping.** Shopify shows a line such as "Free shipping every month" under the delivery method. You can change that text, see [Change the checkout text](#change-the-checkout-text).
- **Renewals are separate orders.** Juo creates every renewal as a new Shopify order, so shipping is calculated again for each renewal.

## Choose your setup

  <Accordion title="Same shipping for everything (default)">
    One-time purchases and subscriptions use the same rates. Renewals use the rates from the product's shipping profile.

    Nothing to set up. This works for most stores.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Different rates for subscriptions">
    For example, free shipping on subscriptions while one-time orders pay for it.

    We create a dedicated shipping profile and link it to your subscription plans. You then add the rates in Shopify under **Settings** → **Shipping and delivery**. Don't add products to this profile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Different rates for renewals than for the first order">
    For example, customers pay shipping on the first order but renewals ship free, or the other way round.

    We can add a renewal-only shipping profile, or keep each subscription on the shipping price from its first order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Free subscription shipping in mixed carts">
    If subscriptions ship free, customers who add a one-time product can still be charged shipping twice, once for each part of the cart.

    We avoid this with an extra checkout profile and a shipping discount from Juo. You'll need to turn off **Split shipping** in Shopify's shipping settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pickup points and parcel lockers">
    Subscribers can receive every renewal at a pickup point, for example InPost via Atlas Pickup Points, BOX NOW or Skroutz Point.

    - In Juo, go to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Atlas Pickup Points**, turn the integration on and set the attribute name your checkout uses, often `InPost Code`.
    - In **Settings** → **General** → **Copy initial order attributes**, add the pickup point attributes so they're copied from the first order to the subscription. See [General settings](/general-settings#copy-initial-order-attributes).
    - BOX NOW and Skroutz Point are turned on per store. Contact us to enable them.

    Subscribers can change their pickup point in the [Customer Portal](/customer-portal). Switching between a pickup point and home delivery may need an update by your team in Juo.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Limitations

- **Conditional rates.** Rates based on order value or weight are checked again on each renewal. A renewal below your free shipping threshold pays shipping, even if the first order didn't. In mixed carts, use a shipping discount instead of conditional rates.
- **Local pickup and local delivery** apply to the whole order. A customer can't pick up one-time items and have the subscription shipped. In-store pickup isn't supported for renewals. See the [FAQ](/FAQ).
- **Carrier-calculated rates.** If one subscription contains products from different shipping profiles, Shopify may calculate the renewal with only one of them.
- **Moving products between profiles.** Existing subscriptions can keep the old rates. Contact us before you move subscription products to another profile.

## Change the checkout text

Shopify writes the recurring delivery line at checkout for you. To change it, go to **Online Store** → **Themes** → **⋯** → **Edit default theme content**, search for **Subscription free with frequency label** and enter your own text. For example: "Free shipping every month on orders over €50".

## Get help with your setup

Unless you keep the default, we set up shipping with you. Email [support@juo.io](mailto:support@juo.io) with:

- the rates you want for one-time orders, first subscription orders and renewals,
- whether customers can mix one-time and subscription products in one cart,
- the carriers, pickup points or shipping apps you use.
