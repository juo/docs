## What are subscription notifications

Subscription notifications are messages sent automatically when something happens to a [subscription](/glossary/subscription). They keep subscribers informed and cut support questions.

## Common notifications

- **Subscription started**: confirmation after the first order.
- **Upcoming renewal**: a reminder a few days before the next order, with a link to skip or change it.
- **Renewal confirmed**: the order was paid and is being prepared.
- **Payment failed**: a request to update the payment method. See [dunning](/glossary/dunning).
- **Out of stock**: a product in the next order is unavailable.
- **Subscription paused or cancelled**: confirmation of the change.

An upcoming-renewal reminder is required by law for some subscriptions in some countries, so check the rules where you sell.
