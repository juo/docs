## What is build-a-box

Build-a-box, also called build your own box or mix and match, is a [subscription box](/glossary/subscription-box) where the customer chooses what goes inside. The merchant sets the range of products and the rules, such as the box size, and the customer fills the box within those rules. The box can be bought once or as a [subscription](/glossary/subscription).

## Build-a-box vs curated box

- **Build-a-box**: the customer decides what's in the box.
- **Curated box**: the merchant decides what's in the box, and the customer gets a surprise or a known selection.

## Why it works

Customers pick what they actually use, so they stay subscribed for longer. Merchants can encourage bigger boxes with tiered discounts, free shipping above a box size or a free gift. This raises [average order value](/glossary/average-order-value) and [lifetime value](/glossary/lifetime-value).
