## What is a subscription box

A subscription box is a [subscription](/glossary/subscription) that delivers a set of products together, usually every month. The value is in the selection and the experience, not only in the products.

## Types of subscription boxes

- **Curated box**: the merchant chooses the products, for example a monthly selection of coffees or snacks.
- [**Build-a-box**](/glossary/build-a-box): the customer chooses the products within rules set by the merchant.
- **Fixed box**: the same set every time, such as a skincare routine.

## How boxes keep subscribers

Rotating products, surprise gifts, tiered discounts for bigger boxes and free shipping keep the box interesting and raise [average order value](/glossary/average-order-value).
