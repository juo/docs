## How retention moves subscription revenue

Subscription revenue comes from a small set of levers:

- **Recurring revenue** = active subscribers × average order value (AOV) × orders per subscriber per period
- **Lifetime value (LTV)** ≈ AOV × gross margin × number of paid cycles before a subscriber leaves
- **Number of paid cycles** grows when fewer subscribers cancel, skip, pause or fail payment in each cycle

Small changes compound. A subscriber kept for one more cycle adds a full order of revenue, and a subscriber kept past an early drop-off point is likely to stay for several more.

## Principles for every retention playbook

- **Lead with value, then offer.** Content that reminds subscribers why they subscribed costs nothing. Incentives cost margin and train subscribers to ask for them.
- **Limit every incentive.** A subscriber shouldn't be able to collect the same offer repeatedly.
- **Test before you scale.** Compare offers or messages on a share of subscribers before you settle on one.
- **Measure the cycles after the save.** A save that cancels at the next renewal only delays churn.
