Go to **Settings** → **Retention and acquisition** in Juo. The same page is also available under **Experiences** → **Retention**.

<Note>
  **Freeze all renewals** has moved to **Settings** → **General**. See [General settings](/general-settings#freeze-renewals).
</Note>

## Dunning flow

Set how long Juo keeps retrying a failed payment before the subscription changes to **Failed**. The period starts from the first billing attempt. The default is 1 month.

For your own retry schedule, emails or actions after a failed payment, use a workflow with the [Payment failed](/workflows/triggers#system-initiated) trigger. See [dunning](/glossary/dunning) for how failed payment recovery works.

## Cancellation flow

The cancellation poll asks subscribers why they want to cancel when they select **Cancel** in the Customer Portal, and responds with an offer.

<Warning>
  If you publish a cancellation workflow, it replaces this poll in the Customer Portal. The poll is used only when no cancellation workflow is published. For new setups, use the [Cancel flow blueprint](/guides/cancel-flow-blueprint).
</Warning>

To set up the poll:

1. Turn on **Enable cancellation poll**.
2. Under **Manage answers**, add the reasons subscribers can choose from.
3. For each answer, choose what happens **When selected**: show a confirmation, offer a discount, ask for a reason, change the delivery interval, offer a skip, a pause, a renewal now or a product swap.
4. To use discounts, turn on **Offer discount on cancellation** and set the percentage, how long it applies (permanently or a number of times) and the message subscribers see.

Results appear under **Analytics** → **Retention flow**. See the [cancellation flow playbook](/playbooks/cancellation-flow) for what to offer and when.

## Upcoming renewal reminders

Set when Juo sends the upcoming renewal event before each renewal. The default is 3 days before. Add more reminders with a label, for example "It's tomorrow" 1 day before, to send several emails.

The events trigger Juo's renewal notifications and are sent to [Klaviyo](/klaviyo-integration) and [Customer.io](/customerio-integration) when connected.

## Quick actions

Set how long one-click links in emails stay valid, such as skip, pause or add product links. The default is 3 days. Keep it at 30 days or less for security.

See [Quick actions](/quick-actions) and [Quick actions for Customer.io](/quick-actions-cio).

## Renewal time

Choose when renewals are processed on their due date:

- **Renew on the initial purchase time**: each subscription renews at the time of day it was first bought.
- **Renew all at the same time**: every renewal on a given day runs at the hour you select.

Processing renewals at one hour makes fulfilment and support easier to plan.

## Subscription intent

Under **Acquisition**, turn on subscription intent to tag customers whose order history suggests they would subscribe. Tagging can take up to 24 hours. See [Subscription intent detection](/subscription-intent-detection).

## SEPA Direct Debit

If your store collects renewals by SEPA Direct Debit, a **SEPA Direct Debit** section appears on this page. See [SEPA Direct Debit](/sepa-direct-debit) for every setting.
