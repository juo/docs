## What is customer lifetime value

Customer lifetime value, shortened to LTV, CLV or CLTV, is how much revenue a [subscriber](/glossary/subscriber) brings in from their first order until they cancel. It's the main measure of how healthy a subscription business is.

## How to calculate LTV

A simple formula for subscriptions:

**LTV = average order value × average number of orders per subscriber**

For example, subscribers who pay €30 per order and stay for 8 renewals on average have an LTV of €240. You can also divide [average order value](/glossary/average-order-value) by your monthly [churn rate](/glossary/churn-rate) to estimate LTV for monthly plans.

## How to increase LTV

- **Keep subscribers longer** with a [cancellation flow](/glossary/retention-flow), skips and pauses instead of cancellations, and loyalty rewards.
- **Raise order value** with upsells, add-ons and [build-a-box](/glossary/build-a-box).
- **Recover failed payments** with [dunning](/glossary/dunning).
