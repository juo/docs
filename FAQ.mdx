## Subscription setup

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I sell a product only as a subscription?">
    On the product page in Shopify, turn on **Only sell this product as a subscription**. The Juo widget then shows only the subscription option. A custom widget may need a small change from your developer to do the same.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't Build-a-box plans show up?">
    Every product in the box must be in the same subscription plans, and all of them must share the **same delivery frequencies**. One mismatched frequency hides the plans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why are there two checkout buttons in the cart?">
    With local payment methods such as iDEAL or BLIK on a store without Shopify Plus, the second button sends customers to the right checkout for those methods. On Shopify Plus, one button is enough.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is the order of delivery frequencies set in the widget?">
    Frequencies appear in the order you added them in Juo. To change the order, add them again in the order you want.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I show the one-time option above the subscription option?">
    Yes. In the theme editor, select the Juo widget and add this under **Custom CSS**:

    ```css
    .j-widget {
      display: flex;
      flex-direction: column-reverse;
    }
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I offer subscriptions only in some countries?">
    Yes. This needs a payment profile connected to subscription orders through the API. Contact us to set it up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do subscriptions work with Shopify Bundles?">
    No. [Shopify Bundles can't use selling plans](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/product-merchandising/bundles#store-configurations). Sell the bundle as its own product or variant, or use [Build-a-box](/build-a-box).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I limit the quantity customers can subscribe to?">
    For regular subscriptions, add quantity validation on the product page and in the cart. In Build-a-box, use its built-in quantity limits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a delivery frequency change the product quantity?">
    Not in Juo itself. Use a pack-size variant per frequency, or a small storefront change that sets quantity and plan together. See [Build a custom subscription storefront](/custom-storefront#offers-that-depend-on-quantity).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I ship a welcome kit first and refills after?">
    Use the **Starter kit → refill swap** [blueprint](/workflows/blueprints). It sends the kit with the first order and replaces it with the refill from the next order. In Juo, go to **Workflows**, open **Blueprints**, choose the blueprint and select **Use this blueprint**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Customers and the portal

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is the Customer Portal different from a Shopify customer account?">
    They're separate, linked by the same email address. Shopify creates the customer at the first purchase, and Juo creates its record at the first subscription. Customers logged in to Shopify can open the portal without logging in again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see the portal exactly as a customer does?">
    Yes. In Juo, open the subscription and select **Generate customer portal link**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does a customer change their delivery address?">
    In the [Customer Portal](/customer-portal), when the subscription uses home delivery. With a pickup point, they change the pickup point there instead. Switching between a pickup point and home delivery is done by your team in Juo.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I merge two Shopify customers when one has an active subscription?">
    Shopify often blocks the merge while a subscription is active. Cancel or move the subscription first. When Shopify does merge them, Juo moves the subscriptions, orders and payment methods to the remaining customer automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I send a customer a link to start a subscription?">
    Use this link, with your own values:

    ```text
    https://your-store.com/cart/add?id=VARIANT_ID&quantity=QUANTITY&selling_plan=SELLING_PLAN_ID
    ```

    For several products:

    ```text
    https://your-store.com/cart/add?items[0][id]=VARIANT_ID_1&items[0][quantity]=QTY_1&items[0][selling_plan]=PLAN_ID_1&items[1][id]=VARIANT_ID_2&items[1][quantity]=QTY_2&items[1][selling_plan]=PLAN_ID_2
    ```

    You can find the selling plan ID by inspecting the frequency dropdown on the product page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which language are notifications sent in?">
    The language the customer used on your store. To change it for one customer, edit their language in Shopify.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change a customer's email address in Juo?">
    No. Juo uses the email from Shopify. Change it in Shopify under **Customers** → the customer → **Edit contact information**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payments and billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use iDEAL or Bancontact without Mollie?">
    No, these subscriptions need Mollie. You can still use Shopify Payments or Stripe for cards, PayPal and one-time purchases. See [Local payment methods](/local-payment-methods) to request access.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does a payment take two days?">
    SEPA payments take **24–48 hours** to complete. To fulfill on time, charge two days before the renewal date. See [Payment timing](/sepa-direct-debit#payment-timing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't discounts work with iDEAL subscriptions?">
    Shopify treats iDEAL and Bancontact orders as one-time purchases. Set the discount to apply to both one-time and subscription purchases.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't iDEAL or Bancontact subscriptions count as recurring revenue in Shopify?">
    Shopify records them as one-time orders, even though they work as subscriptions in Juo. Use [Juo Analytics](/analytics) for subscription numbers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was an order charged back automatically?">
    Some bank accounts don't allow SEPA Direct Debit. Mollie can mark a payment as paid before the bank confirms it, and the bank can reverse it one or two days later. See [Mollie's help article](https://help.mollie.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115000309865-Why-did-my-SEPA-direct-debit-payment-fail) and [SEPA Direct Debit](/sepa-direct-debit).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why are SEPA chargebacks processed automatically, and can I charge again?">
    Customers can ask for a SEPA refund for up to **8 weeks**, and Shopify doesn't allow charging the same order again. Use the [chargeback recovery flow](/sepa-chargebacks) to recover the payment. If the customer asked for the refund, be careful about charging again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add the chargeback payment link to a Klaviyo email?">
    Use the `event.paymentLink` property. You can see it in Klaviyo under **Analytics** → **Metrics** → **Chargeback subscription** → **Activity feed**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can customers update PayPal with Shopify's payment update link?">
    Yes. The Shopify link works for PayPal too.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Discounts and retention

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I give a discount on the first subscription order only?">
    Use a [variable discount](/glossary/variable-discount) in Subscribe and save. A discount code can't be limited to the first order in Juo. That needs a third-party app or custom code.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If a Shopify discount is limited to the first payment, does it affect active subscriptions?">
    Yes. It also applies when added to an active subscription, not only at checkout.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can memberships give discounts or free shipping?">
    Yes. Juo tags members with `membership_active`. Use the tag in Shopify customer segments for discounts or free shipping. Members must be logged in for the benefits to apply. See [Memberships](/memberships).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do Buy X Get Y discounts work with Juo?">
    Shopify's own Buy X Get Y discounts don't work with fixed selling plans. Buy X Get Y discounts from dedicated apps do.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I combine a Build-a-box discount with a discount code?">
    In Shopify, let the code combine with product discounts. In Juo, let the Build-a-box discount combine with order discounts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I reduce cancellations?">
    Start with the [cancellation flow guide](/cancellation-flow). Check **Analytics** → **Cohort retention** to see when subscribers leave, then add [Extras](/extras), [Upsells](/upsells) or loyalty rewards before that point.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Subscriptions and orders

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I edit a subscription?">
    Usually it's renewing and the payment is still processing. You can edit it again once the renewal completes. Also check that:

    - the subscription hasn't got a minimum number of renewals left (you can change its cycle manually),
    - the next renewal date is in the future.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why isn't an estimated order date shown?">
    For cards, the order is placed on the payment date, so there's no estimate. Payment methods that take longer, such as SEPA, show one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do items in one subscription renew on different dates?">
    A subscription can hold items with different frequencies and dates. Each date creates its own order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did the first order include the free item twice?">
    This comes from a custom workflow that includes the parent product in cycle 1. Turn on **Fulfill parent product automatically** to avoid the duplicate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was no order created on the renewal date?">
    This can happen after a migration when Shopify rejects some customer data, such as an email domain. The **Activity log** shows an alert when it happens.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I delete a subscription?">
    No. To stop it from being reactivated, set its status to **Expired**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does removing a selling plan affect active subscriptions?">
    No. Selling plans only control what customers can buy on your store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does deleting a product in Shopify break subscriptions?">
    Yes. [Bulk-swap](/bulk-actions) the product in existing subscriptions first, then delete it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export subscriptions to a spreadsheet automatically?">
    Yes, with the [Juo API](https://juo.dev/).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Shipping and fulfillment

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which shipping method do renewals use?">
    Customers choose the method on the first order. For renewals, Shopify picks the cheapest available method. With equal prices, it picks the first one alphabetically. To change this, rename your methods or ask us for a renewal shipping profile. See [Shipping setup](/shipping-setup).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is the shipping price wrong?">
    Often a location is missing from the shipping profile. Add every location you ship from. For other causes, see the limitations in [Shipping setup](/shipping-setup#limitations).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can subscriptions renew when a product is out of stock?">
    Yes. In Juo, turn on **Settings** → **General** → **Renew subscription when out of stock**. You may also want to turn off the out-of-stock notification under **Settings** → **Notifications**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I stop selling a product but keep its renewals?">
    Unpublish the product from your online store and hide its buy button.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are in-store pickup and pickup points supported?">
    In-store pickup isn't supported for renewals. Carrier pickup points, such as InPost, BOX NOW or Skroutz Point, are. See [Shipping setup](/shipping-setup#choose-your-setup).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does Juo handle several warehouse locations?">
    Shopify decides. An item counts as in stock if any location has it, and Shopify allocates the order across locations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when several subscriptions renew on the same day?">
    Renewals within **24 hours** of each other are merged into one order with one shipping charge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't order tags reach my logistics partner?">
    The tag may be added after the order reaches them. Ask them for a short processing delay. If that doesn't help, add the gift with [Extras](/extras) instead of a tag.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account and integrations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my Juo plan?">
    Uninstall Juo from your Shopify admin. This cancels your Juo plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't Juo events appear in Klaviyo?">
    Events appear only after they happen in Juo. Create a test subscription and skip, pause or change it to send the first events. See [Klaviyo integration](/klaviyo-integration).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why are new subscribers missing from analytics for a day?">
    Usually a time zone difference. Check the time zone in your Shopify store settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
