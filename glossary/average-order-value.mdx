## What is average order value

Average order value, or AOV, is your revenue divided by the number of orders in a period. For subscriptions, it's useful to track AOV separately for first orders and for [renewal orders](/glossary/subscription-order).

**AOV = revenue ÷ number of orders**

## Why it matters for subscriptions

Every extra euro on a renewal repeats on every following renewal. A small increase in AOV has a big effect on [customer lifetime value](/glossary/lifetime-value).

## How to raise AOV

- **Upsells and add-ons** on upcoming renewals, in the customer portal or by email.
- **Bigger boxes** with tiered discounts, for example in [build-a-box](/glossary/build-a-box).
- **Free shipping thresholds** that encourage one more product.
- **One-time add-ons** to the next renewal. See [one-time purchase](/glossary/one-time-purchase).
