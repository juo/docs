## What is a subscription contract

On Shopify, a subscription contract is created when a customer checks out with a [selling plan](/glossary/selling-plan). It stores what the subscription is: products, prices, discounts, [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency), delivery address and method, and the saved payment method.

The subscription app uses the contract to create each [renewal order](/glossary/subscription-order) and keeps it up to date when the subscriber or merchant changes something.

## Why it matters

- Subscriptions live in Shopify, not only in the app, so renewals are regular Shopify orders.
- Some apps and reports read subscription contracts directly, for example in Shopify customer profiles.
- Payment methods saved on contracts can move between apps during a [subscription migration](/glossary/migration).

## Subscription contracts in Juo

Juo manages the contract for every subscription. You see and edit subscriptions in Juo, and Juo keeps the contract in sync.
