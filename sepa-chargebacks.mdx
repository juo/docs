Turn these options on in the [SEPA settings](/sepa-direct-debit#chargeback-recovery). For how SEPA payments and chargebacks work, see [SEPA Direct Debit](/sepa-direct-debit).

## What happens on every chargeback

Whatever your settings, every SEPA chargeback:

1. Refunds the order and tags it `chargeback`.
2. Sets the subscription to **Failed**.
3. Sends a **Chargeback Subscription** event to Klaviyo with the chargeback reason.

## SEPA chargebacks recovery

With **Enable chargeback dunning flow** on, chargebacks with a reason you selected start a recovery flow:

1. Juo creates a new draft order for the failed payment.
2. The **Chargeback Subscription** event sent to Klaviyo includes a Shopify checkout link for that order, in the `event.paymentLink` property.
3. When the customer pays, Juo reactivates the subscription.

Build a Klaviyo flow triggered by **Chargeback Subscription** that asks the customer to complete the payment. See the [Klaviyo integration](/klaviyo-integration).

If the customer asked their bank for the refund, consider whether to ask them to pay again.

## Automatic retries for insufficient funds

For chargebacks with the reason insufficient funds (AM04), turn on **Enable auto-retry via mandate** to retry the payment before contacting the customer:

1. The order is refunded and a draft replacement order is created.
2. Juo retries the payment with the customer's existing SEPA mandate after a delay.
3. If a retry succeeds, the draft order is completed and the subscription continues.
4. If it fails with insufficient funds again, the next retry is scheduled, up to your **Maximum retries**.
5. If every retry fails, the payment method is revoked and the Chargeback Subscription event starts your Klaviyo flow.

Many insufficient-funds chargebacks are temporary, so retries recover part of them without any action from the customer.
