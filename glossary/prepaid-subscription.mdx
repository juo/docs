## What is a prepaid subscription

In a prepaid subscription, the subscriber pays for several deliveries at once and then receives them over time, for example 3 months paid upfront and shipped every month. The billing frequency is longer than the [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency). In a pay-as-you-go subscription, the subscriber pays for every delivery.

## Why offer prepaid plans

- **Cash upfront** and lower [churn](/glossary/churn-rate) during the prepaid period.
- **Gifting**: a prepaid plan makes an easy gift with a clear end.
- **A reason to discount**: subscribers accept a longer commitment for a better price.

The trade-off is a bigger first charge, which some customers won't accept.

## Prepaid subscriptions in Juo

Prepaid is a purchase option of a subscription plan. Set it in the Admin Panel when you create or edit a plan. See [Subscribe and save](/subscribe-and-save).
