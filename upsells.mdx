Juo Upsells let you enhance the subscription experience by offering exclusive, one-time
deals available only to your subscribers.

This guide will walk you through the steps to set up and manage Upsells in the Juo Customer Portal.

## Create an Upsell

To create a new Upsell, navigate to **Experiences → Upsells → Add Upsell** and fill in the details:

1. **Campaign name**: Enter a name for your upsell campaign.
2. **How it's sold**: Choose **Add to subscription** to let subscribers add the products to their subscription or upcoming renewal, or **Buy now as a separate order** to sell them as a one-time order right away. See [Sell an upsell as a separate order](#sell-an-upsell-as-a-separate-order).
3. **Schedule**: Choose whether your upsell should be available always, or only during a specific time period. Set the start and end date for the upsell offer if you choose the latter.
4. **Discount**: Optionally, set a discount percentage. This discount will only be granted to the customers who purchase the product through Juo Upsells.
5. **Usage limit**: Decide if you want to limit the number of products your customers can add using Upsells.
6. **Customer eligibility**: Limit the offer to specific customer segments or customers subscribed to a specific product.
7. Click **Save** button for the other options to appear at the bottom of the page.
8. **Products**: Select which products you want to offer as part of the upsell.
9. Activate the Upsell by pressing the **Active** toggle at the top of the page.

Multiple Upsell offers with different settings can be used together.

## Sell an upsell as a separate order

With **How it's sold** set to **Buy now as a separate order**, subscribers buy the offer products straight away as their own order. Juo charges the order to the subscription's saved payment method, and the subscription doesn't change. In the upsells list, these upsells show **Buy now** as their type.

A buy-now upsell works differently from one added to the subscription:

- **One-time only**: the **Purchase options** section is hidden, because a separate order can't be a subscription.
- **Discount**: choose **Percentage**, **Amount** or **Fixed price**. The discount applies once, to the separate order.
- **Usage limits**: **Once per customer** and **Each product once per customer** count the products a customer bought in earlier buy-now orders.
- **Where it shows**: only in the **Instant product sale** block of the Customer Portal, never in the **Upsell** block or in email upsell links. See [Instant product sale](/editor#instant-product-sale).
- **Who can buy**: only subscribers whose subscription is active and has a payment method.
- **Currency**: products are listed and charged in the subscription's currency.

Each **Instant product sale** block sells one buy-now upsell, which Juo calls a campaign. By default it sells your oldest buy-now upsell that is active and inside its schedule. To sell a different one, pick it under **Upsell campaign** in the block's settings in the [Editor](/editor#instant-product-sale). A customer left out by the campaign's customer eligibility or usage limit sees an empty block, not a different campaign.

## Customer experience

Your selected products will become visible in the Customer Portal as per the schedule.

<Frame caption="Upsells visible in Juo Customer Portal">
  ![Juo Subscription Upsells view in Customer
  Portal](https://cdn.juo.io/content-uploads/Screenshot_2024_09_17_at_14_13_33_9fc4147588.png)
</Frame>

Customers who add Upsell products to their subscription will be charged upon the next renewal, and will receive them along with their scheduled subscription.

Buy-now upsells show in the **Instant product sale** block, under the header of the subscription page. Customers click **Buy now** on a product (the button shows the price), or select several products and click **Review and pay**. After they check the order and click **Place order**, Juo charges the subscription's payment method right away and ships the products as their own order. On the order, the products read **One-time purchase**.
