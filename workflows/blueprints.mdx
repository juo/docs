A blueprint is a ready-made [workflow](/workflows/workflows-overview) built around one subscription goal, such as saving cancellations or rewarding loyal subscribers. You start from the blueprint instead of an empty workflow, complete a short list of to-dos for your store, and publish it.

## What blueprints are for

- **Launch a proven flow quickly.** Each blueprint already contains the triggers, conditions and actions the flow needs.
- **Learn how workflows are built.** Open a blueprint to see how a complete flow fits together, then change it.

## Where to find them

Go to **Workflows** and open the **Blueprints** tab. Blueprints are grouped by the goal they serve. Each card shows how many customizations the blueprint needs, or **Works out of the box** when it needs none, and any integration it relies on, such as Klaviyo.

You can also start from a blueprint when you create a workflow: select **New workflow**, and **Quick start** offers a blueprint before you choose a trigger. When you first open Workflows, the **Welcome to Workflows** screen leads to the blueprints too.

## How a blueprint works

1. **Open a blueprint.** Select a card to see what the blueprint contains, step by step. The blueprint itself is read-only.
2. **Select Use this blueprint.** Juo creates a new draft from it in your workflows. Values that only make sense for one store, such as the products to swap or add, are left empty for you to fill in.
3. **Complete Quick setup.** **Quick setup** walks you through the blueprint's to-dos one at a time, for example setting the discount size, choosing gift products or copying a link into your Klaviyo email. The builder highlights the field to fill in on the canvas. Select **Next** to go on, then check every step in **Review** and select **Complete**.
4. **Publish.** Completing the setup doesn't publish the workflow. Select **Publish** in the top bar when you're ready. **Publish** stays disabled until you reach **Review** or select **Skip setup**.

After publishing, a workflow made from a blueprint behaves like any other workflow. Edit, test or unpublish it the same way.

## Available blueprints

Each blueprint is an optional starting point. You can build the same flow from scratch, or start from the blueprint and change it to fit your store.

## Considerations

- **One published workflow per trigger.** If a blueprint uses a trigger that already has a live workflow, Juo asks **Replace the live workflow?** when you publish. Select **Replace and publish** to make the new workflow live and move the current one back to drafts. To keep both sets of logic, merge them into one workflow instead.
- **Klaviyo blueprints need the email.** Blueprints that start from an email link only work after you add the link to your Klaviyo email. See [Klaviyo integration](/klaviyo-integration).
