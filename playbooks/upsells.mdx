| **Who it's for** | Subscription brands with more than one product subscribers could use |
| **Goal** | Raise average order value by adding products to orders subscribers already receive |
| **Primary metric** | Upsell attach rate: the share of renewal orders with an added product |
| **Supporting metrics** | Average order value, share of add-ons that become recurring, retention of subscribers who add products |

## Why it matters

Every renewal is a purchase from a customer you already know, with no acquisition cost and the shipping already paid for. Adding one product to that order is often the cheapest revenue a subscription brand can earn.

Upsells also help retention. A subscriber who tries more of your range has more reasons to stay, and an order that fits their needs better is less likely to be skipped.

## The strategy

### Where to offer

- **In the Customer Portal.** Subscribers come here to manage their next order, so they're already thinking about what's in it.
- **In the renewal reminder.** The message before each renewal is the natural moment to suggest adding something. One click from the email should add it.
- **In SMS or messaging apps.** Short messages with a direct link work for one clear offer, such as a new flavor or a seasonal product.

### One-time add-on or recurring item

| | One-time add-on | Recurring item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What it is** | Added to the next order only | Becomes part of every order |
| **Best for** | Trying new products, seasonal items, gifts, treats | Products used up at the same pace as the subscription |
| **Effect** | Lifts the next order | Raises the value of every future order |

Start with one-time add-ons. They're an easy yes, because the subscriber isn't committing to anything. When a subscriber adds the same product more than once, invite them to make it part of their regular order.

### What to offer

- **Complementary products** that are used together with what they already receive.
- **Consumables** that run out, such as refills or accessories.
- **New launches and samples**, so subscribers discover the rest of your range.
- **Seasonal or limited items**, where scarcity gives a reason to add now.

Offer a price subscribers can't get elsewhere. The offer should feel like a perk of subscribing, not like an ad.

## Guardrails

- **Keep it short.** Two or three well-chosen products convert better than a full catalog.
- **Limit how often a subscriber sees the same offer**, and how many times they can claim a special price.
- **Check stock** before you promote an item to every subscriber.
- **Make it easy to remove.** A subscriber who added something by mistake should be able to take it out before the order is charged.

## How to measure it

- **Attach rate** per offer and per channel: portal, email, messaging.
- **Average order value** of renewal orders before and after you start offering add-ons.
- **Repeat adds and conversions to recurring items.** They show which products belong in the regular order.
- **Retention** of subscribers who add products compared with those who don't.
