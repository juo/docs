title: "General settings"
description: "Configure inventory, pricing, shipping rates, tags, and order attribute copying for subscriptions."
---

Global settings live under **Settings** in the Admin Panel. This page covers the **General** tab. Related options also appear under **Customer Portal**, **Retention and acquisition**, **Notifications**, and **Integrations**.

## Out of stock items

Choose what happens when a subscription renews with an out-of-stock item:

- **Renew when back in stock** — Hold renewals until stock returns (duration follows your dunning period).
- **Renew when out of stock** — Renew and bill without interruption.

## Price changes

When product prices change in Shopify:

- **Update to the new price** — Upcoming renewals use the new catalog price.
- **Keep the initial price** — Existing subscription lines keep the price from when they were added.

Changes apply to upcoming orders only.

## One-time products price

Control whether selling-plan discounts apply when a customer adds a product as a **one-time** line in the Customer Portal. Enable this if one-time add-ons should receive the same subscription discount as recurring lines.

## Shipping rates

By default, Juo **retains** the shipping rate on a subscription until the customer updates it (for example by changing products or address).

Enable **Dynamic Shipping Rates** to pull rates from your Shopify shipping profile whenever rates change. Already-scheduled upcoming renewals keep their current rate; the new rates apply from the following cycle.

For profile setup details, see [Shipping setup](/shipping-setup).

## Tags

Define tags Juo writes to Shopify **orders** and **customers** (for example first order vs recurring, active vs cancelled). Changes apply to upcoming orders only. Cycle and reference prefixes get an automatic index suffix.

## Copy initial order attributes

Enable this to copy selected custom attributes from the customer’s **first order** onto the subscription — useful when a shipping app adds pickup-point codes after checkout.

- Add the attribute **keys** to whitelist (only those keys are copied).
- Existing subscription attributes are **never overwritten**.

Pair this with pickup-point integrations when attributes must persist onto renewals. See [Shipping setup](/shipping-setup#pickup-points).
