  Shopify's name for a subscription purchase option, such as "Every 2 weeks, save 10%".
</Info>

## What is a selling plan

On Shopify, a selling plan is one way to buy a product other than a regular [one-time purchase](/glossary/one-time-purchase). For subscriptions, it holds the [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency), the billing frequency and the discount.

Selling plans are grouped into **selling plan groups**, which are attached to products. In Juo, a selling plan group is a subscription plan, and each selling plan is one of its frequencies.

## How selling plans are used

- The product page shows the selling plans for the selected variant.
- The cart line carries the chosen selling plan to checkout.
- After checkout, Shopify creates a [subscription contract](/glossary/subscription-contract) from it.

A product with no selling plans can only be bought once. A product set to **Only sell as a subscription** can only be bought with a selling plan.

## Selling plans in Juo

Juo creates selling plans for you when you set up a plan. See [Subscribe and save](/subscribe-and-save). For custom storefronts, see [Build a custom subscription storefront](/custom-storefront).
