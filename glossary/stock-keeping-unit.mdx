## What is a SKU

A SKU, short for stock keeping unit, is a code you give each product variant to track inventory, for example `COF-250-DEC` for a 250 g decaf coffee. Every size, flavor or pack of a product gets its own SKU.

## Why SKUs matter for subscriptions

* **Renewals** ship the exact variant the subscriber chose, so stock and fulfillment depend on correct SKUs.
* **Pack sizes** work best as separate variants with their own SKUs. See [Build a custom subscription storefront](/custom-storefront#offers-that-depend-on-quantity).
* **Migrations** use SKUs or variant IDs to match old products to new ones. See [Migrate to Juo](/migrating-to-juo).
