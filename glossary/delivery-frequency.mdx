## What is delivery frequency

Delivery frequency, or subscription interval, is how often a [subscription order](/glossary/subscription-order) is sent. The merchant offers a few options and the customer picks one at checkout. Subscribers can often change it later in the [Customer Portal](/glossary/customer-portal).

## Delivery frequency vs billing frequency

In a pay-as-you-go subscription, the subscriber pays for every delivery, so both frequencies are the same. In a [prepaid subscription](/glossary/prepaid-subscription), the subscriber pays less often than they receive orders, for example every 3 months for monthly deliveries.

## How to choose your options

Base the options on how long your product lasts. Too frequent leads to a pile-up of product and cancellations. Too rare leaves subscribers running out. Two or three options usually work best.
