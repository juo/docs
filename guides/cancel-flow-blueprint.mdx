When a subscriber clicks **Cancel** in the Customer Portal, the Cancel flow blueprint asks why they want to cancel and responds with an offer that fits the reason: a delay or frequency change, a product swap, or a discount. Subscribers who cancel after their first order get a separate path. Every answer is recorded, so you can review the most common reasons for cancelling.

To decide which reasons and offers fit your store, see [Designing a cancellation flow that saves subscribers](/playbooks/cancellation-flow).

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Blueprint** | Cancel flow |
| **Trigger** | Subscription cancellation requested (`cancellation_clicked`) |
| **Where subscribers see it** | Customer Portal |

## Before you start

- Your [Customer Portal](/customer-portal) is live and linked from your store.
- Check whether you already have a published workflow for cancellations. Only one workflow per trigger can be published, so add the blueprint's logic to the existing workflow or unpublish it first.
- If you use the legacy cancellation poll under **Settings → Retention and acquisition**, decide which one subscribers should see. See [Retention settings](/retention-flow).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from the blueprint">
    Go to **Workflows** and create a new workflow. Choose **Cancel flow** from the blueprints.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the summary popup">
    The first step reminds subscribers what they lose by cancelling. Replace the sample content with your own benefits: savings, convenience, results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the cancellation reasons">
    Edit the poll so the reasons match what your support team hears most. Keep four to six options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the discount size">
    Set the discount offered to subscribers who say the subscription is too expensive. Start small and limit it to one or two orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Review each path, then publish the workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customize for your store

- **New vs long-term subscribers.** The blueprint already separates subscribers after their first order. To add more stages, branch on `subscription.cycle_count` or `subscription.age_days` before the poll. See [Conditions](/workflows/conditions).
- **Product-specific paths.** Branch on `subscription.items contains_where variantId equals …` to give a flagship product its own content and reasons.
- **Limit repeat discounts.** Use a usage limit on the discount offer so a subscriber can't cancel repeatedly to collect it. See [Actions](/workflows/actions).

## Test and launch

1. Open the Customer Portal as a test subscriber and click **Cancel**.
2. Go through every reason and check that the right offer appears and that each path ends with a way to cancel.
3. Accept an offer and confirm the change appears on the subscription.

## Measure

- Review the recorded cancellation reasons in [Analytics](/analytics).
- Track the save rate per reason and the retention of saved subscriptions, as described in [How to measure it](/playbooks/cancellation-flow#how-to-measure-it).
- To compare two offers, add an [experiment](/workflows/experiments).
