## What is a variable discount

A variable discount is a subscription discount that isn't the same on every order. Common setups:

- **Bigger first order discount**: for example 25% off the first order and 10% off every renewal, to win the first purchase.
- **Loyalty discount**: for example 5% off at first, rising to 10% after the third [renewal](/glossary/subscription-renewal), to reward subscribers who stay.

## Why use one

A strong first order discount lowers the barrier to subscribe. A loyalty discount gives subscribers a reason to stay past the first few orders, when most [cancellations](/glossary/subscription-cancellation) happen.
