---
title: "Analytics and reports"
description: "Monitor subscription performance and export metrics from the Admin Panel."
---

Open **Analytics** in the Admin Panel to review performance and export data. Most views share a date range: last **7**, **30**, or **90** days, or a custom range.

## Views

| Tab | What you get |
|---|---|
| **General** | Revenue, subscription orders, AOV, LTV, churn, recurring revenue and forecast |
| **Subscribers** | Active subscribers with Existing / New / Reactivated / Churned breakdown |
| **Products** | Product performance table with CSV export; upsell product performance |
| **Build-a-box** | Build-a-box performance metrics |
| **Cohort retention** | Retention and revenue cohorts by order number or by month; optional product filter |
| **Retention flow** | Cancellation flows triggered, recovered, and cancelled; reasons and recovery-step performance; CSV export |
| **Reports** | Build a custom data report, preview it, then export |

## Reports

On **Analytics → Reports**:

1. Choose a bucket: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Yearly**.
2. Select the columns you need (subscribers, subscriptions, and revenue metrics).
3. **Generate** to preview, then **Export**.

Available column groups include:

- **Subscribers** — Active, Paused, New, Reactivated, Resumed, Churned, LTV, AOV, average orders per customer
- **Subscriptions** — New, Renewals, Cancellations, churn count, cancellation flow started / retained / recovered
- **Revenue** — New subscription, renewals, upsells, and churn revenue

## Activity log

Separate from Analytics, the **Activity log** lists admin and system activity (including [bulk actions](/bulk-actions)). Open a bulk-action entry to review progress and download its log.
