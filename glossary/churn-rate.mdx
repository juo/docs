## What is churn rate

Churn rate is the percentage of [subscribers](/glossary/subscriber) who stop subscribing during a period, usually a month. It's the opposite of retention: a 5% monthly churn rate means 95% of subscribers stay each month.

## How to calculate churn rate

**Churn rate = subscribers lost in the period ÷ subscribers at the start of the period**

For example, if you start the month with 1,000 subscribers and 50 cancel, your monthly churn rate is 5%. Don't count new subscribers from the same month in either number.

## Voluntary and involuntary churn

- **Voluntary churn**: subscribers cancel themselves. Reduce it with a [cancellation flow](/glossary/retention-flow), [skips](/glossary/skip-renewal), [pauses](/glossary/subscription-pause) and [product swaps](/glossary/product-swap).
- **Involuntary churn**: payments fail and aren't recovered. Reduce it with [dunning](/glossary/dunning).

Most subscription churn happens in the first few orders, so look at churn by subscriber age, not only in total.
