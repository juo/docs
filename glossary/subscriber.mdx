## Who is a subscriber

A subscriber is a customer who has bought a [subscription](/glossary/subscription) and receives orders on a schedule. They become a subscriber with their first [subscription order](/glossary/subscription-order) and stop being one when they [cancel](/glossary/subscription-cancellation).

## Subscriber vs customer

A one-time customer decides to buy again every time. A subscriber's next order happens unless they act. That makes subscriber revenue more predictable and usually much higher over time. See [customer lifetime value](/glossary/lifetime-value).

## Active, paused and churned subscribers

- **Active**: the subscription renews on schedule.
- **Paused**: renewals are on hold. See [subscription pause](/glossary/subscription-pause).
- **Churned**: the subscription was canceled or failed. See [churn rate](/glossary/churn-rate) and [win-back](/glossary/win-back).
