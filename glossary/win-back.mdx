## What is win-back

Win-back is a campaign aimed at subscribers who have [cancelled](/glossary/subscription-cancellation). It reaches out after they leave, usually by email or text, with a reason to come back and an easy way to restart.

## What makes a win-back work

- **Timing**: shortly after cancelling, and again when they would have run out of product.
- **Relevance**: an offer that matches why they left, for example a lower [delivery frequency](/glossary/delivery-frequency) for subscribers who had too much product.
- **One click to return**: a link that reactivates the subscription instead of starting over at checkout.

Winning back an existing customer usually costs less than finding a new one.
